Recipes for cooking various cereal dishes

Cereals can be used to prepare a wide range of dishes including meat pilau, vegetable rice, maize and kidney beans, loshoro, fried vermicelli and Chelsea buns.

Meat pilau

Ingredients (serve 2 people)

250 g rice

100 g meat

6 g pilau masala

1 g curry powder

50 ml cooking oil

4 cloves of garlic

3 onions

2 g salt

Water

1 tomato

Method

1. Wash and cut the meat into small pieces.

2. Boil the meat with little salt until it is slightly tender.

3. Remove unwanted particles from the rice and wash it.

4. Slice the onion thinly.

5. Peel and crush the garlic.

6. Fry the onions until they are slightly brown. Add garlic and other spices.

Continue stirring them until they attain a brown colour.

7. Add boiled meat without stock and continue stirring for 5-7 minutes.

8. Add rice and stir it for 2-3 minutes.

9. Add meat stock and enough water for cooking the rice. Add the remaining salt and allow it to simmer. Stir occasionally. Cook for about 15–20 minutes, or until the rice is soft and all liquid is absorbed.

10. Serve it on a plate and garnish it with slices of tomato or any suitable garnish as shown in Figure 2.3.
