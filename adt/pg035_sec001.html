Cereal

Common uses

Rice

Making main dishes such as vegetable rice, cooked rice with beans, pilau; rice flour used for puddings, baked goods, and snacks

Barley

Used for brewing beer and malted drinks; barley grains can be used in making soups

Sorghum

Used for making porridge, stiff porridge, breads, and cookies

Millet

Used for making porridge and stiff porridge, cakes, biscuits, and breads

Oats

Used for making porridge, cakes, and biscuits; thickening soups and stews

Methods of cooking cereals

Cereals can be prepared using a variety of cooking methods. Most of them are easy to apply. Each method has an impact on the final texture, flavour and nutritional quality of the cereal dish prepared. Most cereals, such as rice, are cooked by boiling.

Other methods include baking, frying and pressure cooking. For example, rice is partially cooked by boiling and then simmering it until it is fully cooked. Pressure cooking can be used for cooking foods that are hard to cook like maize and kidney bean dish. Baking can also be used, especially in preparing wheat based products like bread and buns while frying is often employed in the preparation of food items such as buns, chapati, doughnuts, and cereal-based fritters.
