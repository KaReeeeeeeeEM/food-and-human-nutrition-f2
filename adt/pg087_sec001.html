Banana with meat stew

Ingredients (serve 2 people)

100 g meat steak

4 medium-size green banana

250 ml coconut milk

1 green pepper

1 carrot

1 onion

15 ml vegetable oil

4 g salt

Water

Coriander leaves

Method

1. Slice the tomato and chop the onion and carrot.

2. Wash the meat, cut it into cubes and then boil it.

3. Peel and cut the bananas into pieces and put them in a basin of cold water.

4. Prepare coconut milk.

5. Sauté onion in the vegetable oil.

6. Add the cooked meat, the pieces of bananas, carrot, salt and water or stock.

The liquid should cover the mixture.

7. Simmer the bananas until they are tender.

8. Add the coconut milk while continuing to stir slowly to prevent curdling and let it simmer for about five minutes.

9. Serve it on a plate and garnish with sliced green pepper or any suitable garnish as shown in Figure 3.13.
