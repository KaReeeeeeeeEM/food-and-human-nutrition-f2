(vii) Family planning

Family planning allows individuals and couples to plan and attain their desired family size through child spacing. This is achieved via different methods of family planning such as using condoms and calendar-based methods. Family planning

promotes

good

nutrition

because it allows sufficient time for child care before having another one.

Optimal birth spacing allows mothers to have more time, energy and resources to breastfeed and feed their infants and children adequately. It also provides recovery of maternal nutrient stores and health hence allowing a woman to be healthy before another pregnancy.

A successful family planning programme is likely to have a positive effect on nutrition and care of the mother, child and future family.

(viii) Physical activity

Refers to any body movement that involves muscles and it uses energy. It makes a person feel better with more energy, better mood and better sleep.

The person will also feel more relaxed.

Physical activity also improves the functioning of body systems such as the

digestive and respiratory systems. The type of physical activity chosen should include some forms of aerobic exercise.

Aerobic exercise is a type of exercise that uses oxygen to improve the ability of the body to burn fat. It improves heart rates and the pumping of blood thus reduces the risk of heart diseases. Aerobic exercise includes walking, jogging, swimming,

performing

household

chores and dancing. Physical activity is important for achieving proper energy balance, which is needed to prevent obesity. Regular physical activity also lowers the risk of overweight and obesity.

Ways of managing malnutrition

Malnutrition is caused by multiple factors thus various approaches need to be considered for its management particularly in children under the age of 5 years. Depending on the severity of malnutrition, the management of children with acute malnutrition can be done through attendance to health facility, nutrition rehabilitation , use of therapeutic foods and provision of supplementary foods later when the child recover, management can continue at home.
