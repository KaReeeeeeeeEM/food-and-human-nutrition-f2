Medium batter: This type of batter can be used for coating foods such as fish, meat, chicken, potato balls and liver snacks. Such foods are coated to protect them from intensive heat, prevent nutrient and juice loss, prevent food breakage and improve food appearance. It is better to season the food before coating to add flavour or marinade it to make it tender before coating.

Use the following proportions in making medium batter:

250 g wheat flour

250 ml milk or water

2 eggs

4 g baking powder

2.5 g salt

Thick batter: This type of batter is used to make dishes such as banana fritters, sausage fritters and pineapple fritters. Other foods that can be made from thick batter include rice burns and bhajia.

Use the following proportions in making a thick batter.

250 g flour

150 mls milk or coconut milk or water

2 eggs

4 g baking powder

2.5 g salt
