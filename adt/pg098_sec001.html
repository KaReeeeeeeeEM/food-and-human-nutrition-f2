Dish meal and course meal

Meals can either be prepared as a dish meal or a course meal. A dish meal refers to a single prepared food item or a combination of ingredients served together as one dish. It is common in informal or everyday settings due to its simplicity and convenience. It can be a complete meal by its own or just one part of a larger meal as follows.

(i) One dish meal: This is a meal where all ingredients are cooked together in one pot, for example, banana with meat stew or meat pilau.

(ii) Two dish meal: This meal has two dishes prepared in separate cooking pots, for example, vegetable rice and meat stew.

(iii) Three dish meal: This meal consists of three separate dishes each cooked in its own pots, for example, boiled rice, liver stew and fried amaranths.
