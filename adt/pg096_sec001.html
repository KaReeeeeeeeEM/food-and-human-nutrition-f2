Effective meal planning ensures efficient use of materials, time and financial resources while maintaining the desired quality.

A well-planned, properly cooked and thoroughly served meal should not only be nutritious but also enjoyable and suitable for all individuals.

Family meals

Family meals are meals shared by family members to cater for daily nutritional needs. These meals are more than just nourishment as they foster connection, strengthen relationships and promote health eating habits. A family overall health and well-being depends on how its members are fed. Thus, when planning family meals, it is essential to include all necessary nutrients required by the body from various food groups.

Typical family meals include breakfast, lunch, supper or dinner, which may consist of one or more course meals.

Snack and bites can sometimes be eaten in-between family meals.

Breakfast

Breakfast, as the name suggests, is the meal that ‘breaks the fast’ after a night sleep. It is typically the first meal of the day taken in the morning and it plays a vital role in restoring energy levels and supplying essential nutrients to start the

day. A well-balanced breakfast supports physical and mental performance, making it an important meal that should not be skipped. While breakfast may vary by culture and preference, it should contain all the necessary nutrients. Hot drinks such as tea, cocoa and coffee with or without milk, spiced water or soup can also be included in the breakfast meal to stimulate and make the body active.

Lunch

Lunch is a meal prepared and eaten during mid-day. It is the second meal of the day after breakfast and it plays a crucial role in replenishing energy and

supplying

essential

nutrients

needed to sustain individuals through the afternoon. A well-balanced lunch supports concentration, physical activity and productivity during the second half of the day.

Dinner

This is a formal evening meal typically prepared at home for the family. It may also be served on special occasions such as graduations, anniversaries, birthdays or dinner parties. It is considered the main and often heaviest meal of the day, typically served in the evening and often consists of multiple courses.
