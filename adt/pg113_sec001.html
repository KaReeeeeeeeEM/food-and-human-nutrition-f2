Meals for people with diabetes

Meals for diabetic people should provide essential nutrients to meet their requirements. When planning meals for these people, one should consider meals that cause a slower and lower rise in blood sugar levels. The following are points to consider when planning for meals for diabetic patients.

(i)

The meal must be balanced.

However, control the intake of carbohydrates or starchy foods such as refined rice, sweet potatoes, table sugar, honey and fruits with high sugar content.

These foods can quickly shoot

the blood sugar levels up.

(ii) The meal should contain more protein foods such as fish, lean meat, soybeans, peas and yogurt.

(iii) The

meal

should

include

fibre-rich foods and plenty of vegetables such as cabbage, amaranths,

whole

grains

and pulses. The fibre slows down glucose absorption thus regulating blood sugar levels.

(iv) Reduce the use of added sugar foods such as refined grains.

(v) Plan

for

cooking

methods

that use low or no fat such as steaming, poaching, boiling and grilling.

(vi) The meal should be flavourful, with herbs and spices, and served attractively to meet their appetite.

Suggested suitable dishes for people with diabetes

Whole grain breakfast cereals with skimmed milk, vegetable salads, boiled cabbage, sautéed mixed vegetables, stewed green grams, poached fish, spiced tea without sugar, avocado with yogurt smoothie, braised peas, grilled lean meat, cucumber with carrot juice, boiled brown rice, finger millet stiff porridge, grilled chicken, and whole grain toasted sandwich with vegetables.
