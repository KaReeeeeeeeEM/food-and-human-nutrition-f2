Method

1. Sort and wash the beans thoroughly. Then, boil them until they become soft.

2. Peel the potatoes, wash and cut them into small cubes.

3. Slice the onion, fry it and then add potatoes and salt.

4. Add milk (if needed) and water then leave to cook; when almost soft, add cooked beans and simmer them for 10 minutes.

5. Mash the mixture until they become soft and then serve the food.

Spinach soup

Ingredients (serve 2 people)

A bunch of spinach leaves

3 g butter

500 ml water or stock

5 g corn flour

1 egg (optional)

2 g salt

Method

1. Wash the spinach.

2. Heat the butter and toss the spinach leaves in it.

3. When the spinach leaves become partially cooked, add some water or stock, salt and spices and cook for 5 minutes.

4. When the spinach is cooked, blend it to obtain a thin paste and return it to the pan.

5. Mix water and corn flour then add the mixture to the thin paste while stirring to obtain soup. Cook for 5 – 10 minutes. Add more seasonings for taste, if necessary.

6. Beat the egg yolk in a separate dish and slowly pour the hot soup over it while stirring the mixture.

7. Serve it while hot.
