Glossary

Antioxidants

are natural substances that protect the body’s cells from damage caused by harmful molecules that increase the risk of diseases like cancer

Aroma

is a noticeable and usually pleasant smell of the food

Body Mass Index

is an approximate measure of whether an individual is overweight or underweight

Bouquet garni

is a bundle of herbs usually tied with string and mainly used to prepare soup, stock, casseroles and various stews

Cocktail

are small pieces of cold food that can easily and quickly be put together around a sauce, usually with tangy flavoured, served as an appetizer

Conception

is the beginning of pregnancy marked by fertilization of woman’s egg by a sperm

Dehulled maize

is the maize grain which its outer layer is removed by abrasion during polishing process

Diabetes mellitus

is a disease that affects how the body uses blood sugar (glucose)

Food fortification

is the practice of adding vitamins and minerals to commonly consumed foods during processing to increase their nutrients value

Gluten

is a protein present in cereal grains especially wheat, barley and rye which, when combined with water, form the elastic dough
