Unpolished rice: This type of rice does not undergo extensive milling as a result, the bran and germ rich in B vitamins, minerals and protein remain intact. This type of rice has a dull brown colour. It is commonly referred to as brown rice or whole grain rice and it is more nutritious. However, it is less commonly consumed due to the unpalatable texture of the bran. Additionally, unpolished rice has a shorter shelf life because the fat content in the germ is susceptible to oxidation over time.

Sorghum

Sorghum grains are typically small, round with a slightly flattened or elliptical shape. Their colour varies by variety, ranging from white to red, brown or even black. Sorghum is a drought-tolerant crop and versatile cereal grain. It is rich in carbohydrates, mainly starch. It is a good source of dietary fibre and protein.

Additionally, sorghum provides essential minerals like potassium and B vitamins such as thiamine and niacin.

Millet

Millet is a small round generally tiny grain. The most widely grown millet in Tanzania includes finger millet and pearl millet. Their plants can withstand harsh climatic factors such as unpredictable climate and they can flourish in nutrient-depleted soils. Thus, they are considered resilient in difficult climatic conditions. Millet is a good source of carbohydrates and protein. The essential amino acid profile of millet protein is better than that of other cereals such as maize. Millet is also rich in calcium, phosphorous, magnesium, vitamin E, K and B3 (Niacin).
