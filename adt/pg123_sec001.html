Food and Human Nutrition

Form Two

Germination

is sprouting of cereal and legume grains brought about by soaking them in water. This process helps to reduce the effect of anti-nutritional factors and improve the quality of nutrients

Haemoglobin

is a protein inside red blood cells that carries oxygen from the lungs to tissues and organs in the body and it carries carbon dioxide back to the lungs

Hors d’oeuvres

is a savoury dish served in small portion as an appetizer

Husk

is an outer layer of various seeds or fruits such as barley and maize

Immunization

is the process whereby an individual is given a weakened form of a microorganism that helps the body to fight against infections or diseases

Intelligent Quotient

is a measure of a person’s reasoning ability

Mycotoxin

is the toxic substance produced by fungus which mostly grows on cereals, nuts, dried fruits or spices

Pickles

are fruits or vegetables that are preserved and flavoured by soaking in vinegar or brine or through fermentation giving them a sour or salty taste

Osteoporosis

is a bone disease that develops when the structure and strength of bone changes leading to a decrease in bone strength that can increase the risk of broken bones

Punch

is a refreshing non-alcoholic mixture of fruit juices often with carbonated soda added
