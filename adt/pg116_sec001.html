The number of guests: The meal should be enough for all the guests.

There should be extra meals in case of unexpected guests.

The venue: It is important to consider the venue for the occasion. The venue will help to set the table and prepare crockery, cutlery and the type of service that can be offered to the guests.

The type of occasion: Every occasion requires certain types of food. The food should be planned wisely to suit the type of occasion. For example, cakes and biscuits are good for birthday parties.

Cooking equipment: Each occasion requires special cooking-ware and utensils for preparing, cooking and serving food. Such ware and utensils will depend on the type of meals to be prepared, the number of guests and the type of services to be served. Such cooking equipment and utensils include slicers, cooking pots, serving spoons and serving bowls.

Type of service: The service to be offered depends on the kind of occasion such as a cocktail or buffet. The type of service will determine the equipment to use and the dishes to prepare. Therefore, a suitable style of serving dishes should be chosen based on the type of occasion, venue and the number of guests to be served.

Birthday party meals

The meal for a birthday party should contain foods that can attract the guests to eat. It should comprise a variety of dishes to make a balanced diet. Foods should be light, easy to chew, colourful and delicious. Suitable dishes for birthday parties include birthday

cakes,

roasted

chicken,

samosas, assorted drinks and punch.

Wedding party meals

These meals are served at wedding parties. Wedding party meals include both dry and moist foods. The guests can choose meals of their choice. Such meals should have a variety of colours and they should be served attractively according to the desired type of service. Suitable dishes for wedding parties are wedding cakes, fruit and vegetable salads, bites, meat or peas pilau, banana meat stew, assorted drinks and punch.

Children’s party meals

Children prefer to eat multi-coloured foods. Their food should be delicious, soft and appealing. Suitable dishes include light bites, ice-cream, soft drinks, fruits and vegetable salads, non-alcoholic punches and roasted nuts.
