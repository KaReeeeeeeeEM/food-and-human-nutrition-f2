It contains antinutrients that reduce nutrient absorption. Wholemeal flour contains antinutrients, which are naturally occurring compounds in foods that can interfere with the absorption or utilisation of nutrients, potentially reducing their nutritional value. For example, phytic acid, commonly found in cereals, may impair the absorption of minerals such as zinc, calcium, and iron by forming insoluble compounds with them.

It affects the quality of certain baked products. It does not produce good-quality baked products, particularly for items like cakes and pastries. The high fibre content in this flour interferes with gluten development and it affects texture, often resulting in denser, less tender baked goods with reduced volume.

It is more susceptible to mycotoxin contamination. Mycotoxins are naturally occurring toxic substances produced by fungus. In cereals, mycotoxins tend to be mostly concentrated in the bran. Therefore, wholemeal flour is more susceptible to contamination by mycotoxins because it is processed by using unrefined cereals.

Poor storage conditions remain the major cause of fungal contamination hence increasing the risk of mycotoxin contamination in cereals. If consumed in amounts exceeding safe limits, they can lead to adverse health effects such as acute poisoning.

Proper handling and storage of cereals are essential to minimize this risk.

(ii) White flour

White flour is also known as polished flour. It is highly milled and sieved to remove the outer layers (bran) and germ. Approximately 30 per cent of the grain, mainly the bran and germ, is removed during milling. Hence, only 70 percent of the original grain remains. White flour lacks essential nutrients such as calcium, iron, B vitamins, zinc, niacin, copper and magnesium, which are predominantly found in the bran and germ that are removed during milling. Fortification can be applied in the refined flour to restore the missing nutrients.

Advantages of white flour

The following are the advantages of white flour.

It has improved baking qualities. White flour derived from wheat has improved baking qualities. It produces baked products with a fine texture and light appearance thus making it ideal for breads, cakes and pastry dishes.

It contains reduced quantities of antinutrients. This flour has fewer antinutritional compounds such as phytic acid, which are mostly removed during refining hence nutrients absorption in this type of flour is easier.
