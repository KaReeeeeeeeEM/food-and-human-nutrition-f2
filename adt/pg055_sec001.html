Food and Human Nutrition

Form Two

Banana fritters

Ingredients (serve 3 people)

250 g wheat flour

2 eggs

2 g salt

150 mls milk or water

4 g baking powder

6 ripe bananas

50 g icing sugar

500 mls cooking oil

Method

1. Prepare a thick batter.

2. Peel each banana and cut it in half lengthwise.

3. Coat the cut banana with batter.

4. Heat the oil until moderately hot. Add the coated bananas and fry them until they become golden brown in colour.

5. Drain them to remove excess oil.

6. Serve them on a plate and dredge them with icing sugar as shown in Figure

2.13.
