Time of a day

Meals should be planned based on the appropriate time of the day such as morning, afternoon, and evening. For example, supper should be light because a person is preparing to go to bed thus little energy is required for the functions of the body.

Religion, cultural values and traditional beliefs

Religion, cultural values and traditional beliefs determine what should be cooked and eaten at a particular time. In planning for meals, one should consider these beliefs to prepare meals that are acceptable in the family.

Dietary preferences and restrictions

Some family members prefer a certain kind of food while others do not.

It is important to consider family preferences. This will also prevent food loss when unpreferred food is prepared.

Individual restrictions and allergies should also be considered including individuals who are vegetarians.
