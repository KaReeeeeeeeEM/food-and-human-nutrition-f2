Figure 2.9: Finger millet porridge

Maize sweet

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

250 g maize flour

250 ml milk

1 egg

2 g ground cinnamon (optional)

60g butter or margarine

100 g sugar

Water

Method

1. Pre-heat the oven to 120°C.

2. Sieve the flour into a bowl. Add sugar and cinnamon.

3. In a separate bowl, beat the egg and mix it with milk and melted butter. Add to the flour mixture and mix them well.

4. More water may be added at a time into the mixture to obtain a thick paste.

5. Pour the mixture into a greased baking tin and bake in a moderately hot oven at the temperature between 130°C and 150°C for 1 hour.

6. Serve it hot or cold.
