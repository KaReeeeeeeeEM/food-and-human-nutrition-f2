Nutritive value of batter

The nutritive value of batter depends on the ingredients used to make it. The nutrients found in batter are carbohydrates due to the presence of flour, protein due to the presence of eggs as well as milk and fats due to the addition of milk or coconut milk.

It also has minerals and vitamins, depending on the type of flour used.

Recipes for making different dishes using batter

Batter can be used to make various dishes including pancakes (thin batter), liver snacks (medium batter) and banana fritters (thick batter).

Pancake

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

125 g wheat flour

180 mls milk or water

1 egg

2g salt

150 mls cooking oil

3 leaves of lettuce

1 green pepper

1 onion

1 carrot

Method

1. Prepare a thin batter.

2. Leave the batter to stand for 15 minutes to allow starch grains to absorb water.

3. Slice onions and green pepper thinly and grate carrot. Add them to the batter then stir them well.

4. Add a little oil to the frying pan.

5. Heat the frying pan with oil until it is hot for frying.
