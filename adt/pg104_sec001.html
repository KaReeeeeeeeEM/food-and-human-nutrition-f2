Food and Human Nutrition

Form Two

(i) The meal should be balanced with more animal proteins such as meat, fish, milk and eggs since they contain quality proteins, vitamins and minerals which are easily absorbed in the body.

These nutrients help the body to recover from diseases and protect it against infectious diseases.

(ii) The meal should be responsive to the signs and symptoms experienced by a HIV and AIDS individual. For example, soft liquid foods rich in mineral salts such as soup, beverages and milk dishes are suitable for a patient with a fever and diarrhoea conditions.

(iii) Fruits and vegetables should be part of their main meal since they contain many antioxidants, and micronutrients which protect the body against diseases and strengthen its immune system.

(iv) Their meals should include soft and easily digested foods.

Avoid using too much fat as it is difficult to digest.

Suggested suitable dishes for people with HIV and AIDS

Scrambled egg, stewed meat, roasted chicken, grilled fish, banana with meat stew, vegetable rice, vegetable salad,

braised mixed vegetables, mixed fruit salad, banana milk shake, mixed fruit juices, cocoa with milk, enriched porridge, vegetable soup, spaghetti with minced meat, stewed lentils, pumpkin leaves with groundnuts sauce, bread sandwich with eggs and vegetables.

Meals for manual workers

Manual

workers

are

individuals

involved in heavy works. Examples of manual workers include masons, farmers and mechanics. These people spend more energy on their work. Their meals should provide enough calories to meet their energy needs and proteins to build and maintain strong muscles.
