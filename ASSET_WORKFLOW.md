# Asset Workflow

- The copied source PDF, extracted images, crop versions, fonts, cache, LLM logs, generated web assets, and audit records remain inside this book directory.
- Genuine photography and complex illustrations may remain raster assets. Text, headings, callouts, tables, leaders, and simple structures must remain semantic HTML/CSS/SVG rather than page-wide screenshots.
- Image crops must preserve source orientation, bounds, captions, and surviving version IDs used by fixed-layout draw items.
- Composite page regions must not visibly duplicate extracted text or produce duplicate narration.
- Temporary contact sheets and diagnostic screenshots are disposable and kept outside the repository.
